Common questions

Do you work with early-stage teams?

Yes. Pre-seed to Series B is our focus. If you're pre-product, we'll start with one core flow.

Can you work with our existing design system?

Yes. We'll audit what you have, extend it where it's thin and fix the parts that have drifted from your code.

What do you need from us?

Product access, one person who can make decisions, and about two hours a week for reviews.

Do you write front-end code?

Yes. We work in your repo, build the UI in your stack and open pull requests your engineers review and merge. If you'd rather build it yourselves, we'll hand off Figma files and specs instead.

Is it safe to let you into our codebase?

We work on branches and never push to main. Every change goes through your normal review and CI, and your team decides what merges.
