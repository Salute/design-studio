Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cozy the right fit for us?

Probably, if you're a funded B2B SaaS team between pre-seed and Series B with a dashboard, admin tool or internal product that has outgrown how it looks and behaves. We work best where the screens are dense and the data is the point.

We're a weaker fit for marketing sites, brand identity from scratch, or consumer apps that live or die on a single onboarding screen.

Can we work with our existing design system?

Yes. We audit what you already have, extend it where it's thin, and fix the parts that have drifted from your code. If anything is already shipped, starting from scratch is usually the wrong move — and we'll say so.

Why is there no client work on the site?

Cozy is a new studio, so we're not showing client work yet, and we're not going to invent any. Instead we'll walk you through how we work and scope a small first project — so you can judge the work itself rather than a portfolio page.

What do you need from us?

Product access, one person who can make decisions, and us added to Slack. We spend the first day learning your product; everything after that runs on the shared board.

Do you write front-end code?

No. We design to your stack, write specs your engineers can build from, and stay reachable during the build rather than disappearing at handoff.
